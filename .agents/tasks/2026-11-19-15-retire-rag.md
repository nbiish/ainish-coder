## Findings that shape the work

- The corpus is **340,322 tokens.** A 1M window holds it three times over, so a vector index was
  never buying reach — the prime is 4K tokens and something still has to choose what goes in it.
- The Rust crate is the **only** implementation that signs `.agents/wiki/ledger.md`. Deleting it
  without porting that would silently drop signed governance history.
- `pqc-secrets vault sign <FILE>` exists and writes a detached `.sig`, which is what the port needs.
- CI never audits the crate; the lockfile manifest does pin it.
- The stale compiled binary still runs and regenerates the wiki — reproduced during planning, which
  is why the binary is deleted in the same commit that removes the crate.
