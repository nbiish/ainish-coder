### [2026-11-05T03:05:00Z] skills-selection-regression
`--rules` silently deployed zero `.agents/skills/` packs because `b268b75` removed the interactive selector (to stop a WSL stall) and `fdd8537` then defaulted unrecorded packs to OFF — leaving no way to record a selection from the rules path. Fixed by asking once per repo on a real TTY, then applying the saved selection; `--select-skills` reopens the choice and `--skills-list [--json]` reports it. The selector's key read is now bounded by `AINISH_SKILLS_PROMPT_TIMEOUT` (default 120s) and a cancel/timeout falls back to the saved selection, so the stall that motivated the removal cannot recur through this path.
